About us

About The Learner Base

We believe students do their best work when someone teaches them, not a crowded room. Every brand we build starts from that idea.

The Learner Base creates and runs learning brands. Each one has its own specialism and its own team of tutors, and all of them share the same standards for tutor vetting, curriculum alignment and honest reporting to families.

  • Vetted specialists

    Tutors are screened for subject expertise, teaching experience and communication before they meet a student.

  • Curriculum first

    Lessons follow each student's own syllabus, from IB and Cambridge to CBSE and UK entrance exams.

  • Visible progress

    Parents receive attendance records, lesson notes and clear next steps.

What our tutors teach

International Baccalaureate

A-Levels

CBSE

IT & programming
Structured tracks such as Python programming for beginners.
Competitive mathematics
