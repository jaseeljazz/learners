A learning company built around one tutor and one student.

The Learner Base creates and runs learning brands. Our flagship, Neural Bridge Campus, delivers one-to-one online tuition for students around the world.

Our story

Our story

We believe students do their best work when someone teaches them, not a crowded room. Every brand we build starts from that idea.

The Learner Base began with a simple observation: most students don't need more content, they need someone who notices exactly where they are stuck. So we build learning brands around that kind of attention.

Each brand has its own specialism and its own team of tutors. All of them share the same standards for tutor vetting, curriculum alignment and honest reporting to families.

  • Vetted specialists

    Tutors are screened for subject expertise, teaching experience and communication before they meet a student.

  • Curriculum first

    Lessons follow each student's own syllabus, from IB and Cambridge to CBSE and UK entrance exams.

  • Visible progress

    Parents receive attendance records, lesson notes and clear next steps.
