Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how The Learner Base ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit this website or contact us. Lessons and enrolment are handled by Neural Bridge Campus, which has its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, email address, phone number and message when you use our contact form or email us.
  • Technical information: basic data such as browser type, device and pages visited, collected automatically to keep the site working and secure.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and requests.
  • To discuss partnerships, media enquiries or work opportunities you contact us about.
  • To maintain, secure and improve this website.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing your information

We share information only when needed: with our learning brands (such as Neural Bridge Campus) if your enquiry is about lessons, with service providers who help us run the website and email, or when the law requires it.

Cookies

This website may use essential cookies and similar technologies so that pages load correctly. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as needed to answer your enquiry and meet legal or accounting requirements, then delete it securely.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to how we use it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for parents, partners and tutors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@thelearnerbase.com or use our contact form.
